# Iggy Meadow — V50 Visual Art Bible

## North star
Iggy Meadow should read as a polished contemporary virtual-pet world: soft, bright, collectible, kind, and screenshot-worthy. The goal is not vintage clip art and not a literal copy of another pet site. It is an original world with the density and care of a long-running virtual-pet game.

## Italian Greyhound anatomy
Every visible Iggy must read immediately as an Italian Greyhound.

- long, narrow muzzle
- rose ears with visible inner-ear softness
- deep chest and dramatic tuck-up
- long fine legs with small paws
- slim neck and elegant silhouette
- whip tail
- expressive but not oversized rodent-like face
- large warm eyes without chibi deformation

If an Iggy reads as a rat, mouse, generic dog, or capybara, the asset fails review.

## Rendering standard
- painted / polished digital illustration rather than flat procedural geometry
- clean soft edges and readable silhouette at small sizes
- subtle material texture, not noisy grain
- modern pastel fantasy palette
- crisp highlights and gentle contact shadows
- no sepia wash over the whole interface
- no faux-aged parchment as the default surface

## Core palette
- Cream: #FFFAF2
- Sky: #DFF2FF
- Mint: #DFF3E7
- Blush: #F8DFE9
- Lilac: #E9E2FB
- Forest: #385A4A
- Ink: #4B465A
- Gold accent: #D8AF62

Brown/wood tones are accents for furniture and frames, not the dominant UI color.

## Character hierarchy
1. The Iggy is the focal point.
2. Environment supports the Iggy.
3. UI supports the environment.
4. Decoration never compensates for weak character art.

## UI rules
- generous rounded panels with light borders and soft shadows
- bright surfaces rather than heavy brown frames
- high-information density is allowed, but grouped into clear cards
- buttons should feel tactile without looking like 1990s beveled HTML controls
- important pet art should be larger than icon art
- recurring NPCs use the same anatomy standard as player Iggies

## Wardrobe
- complete outfits use painted full-body artwork
- partial accessories should never force the pet back to an inferior procedural body
- temporary accessory badges are acceptable during the transition, but the dog body stays painted

## Quality gate
A screen is not visually complete merely because it contains graphics. It is complete only if the team would be comfortable using a screenshot of that screen to advertise Iggy Meadow.
